# 🚀 MCT India Summit Frontier Hack Event

Welcome to the **MCT India Summit Frontier Hack Event**! This hack is designed **by MCTs, for MCTs**—focusing on the real challenges Microsoft Certified Trainers face **before**, **during**, and **after** training delivery.

This is not about building a perfect product. It’s about **problem framing, collaboration, and ideation** using Copilot and AI as thinking partners.

---

## 🎯 Goal

Accelerate alignment on the *Frontier Firm* mindset by:

- Identifying high‑impact challenges faced by **Microsoft Certified Trainers**
- Exploring how **AI and Copilot** can reduce friction in training delivery
- Producing clear, actionable solution ideas that can scale across the MCT community

The emphasis is on **thinking clearly, collaborating effectively, and experimenting boldly**.

---

## 🔄 Event Flow (70 minutes)

- **Kickoff & Team Formation** – Meet your table and coach  
- **Define the Challenge** – Identify a real MCT pain point  
- **Break Down the Problem** – Understand root causes and impact  
- **Explore AI Solutions** – Ideate with Copilot and Researcher  
- **Make the Case** – Select and justify the best solution  
- **Submit Your Idea** – Finalize and share your output  

---

## 🧩 The Problem Space (MCT‑Focused)

Your problem statement **must be grounded in real issues faced by MCTs**, such as:

### Pre‑Delivery
- Courseware ramp‑up time and content overload  
- Customizing labs, demos, and case studies  
- Staying current with rapidly evolving Microsoft products  

### In‑Delivery
- Learner engagement and attention fatigue  
- Managing mixed‑skill audiences  
- Time management during live training  

### Post‑Delivery
- Learner retention and follow‑up support  
- Reusable assets and content versioning  
- Feedback analysis and continuous improvement  

Your challenge should be **specific, observable, and worth solving**.

---

## 🛠 How You’ll Work

- Use **Copilot (Work mode)** to surface insights from your own context  
- Balance **AI suggestions with team discussion**—neither replaces the other  
- Capture all outputs in the **Hack Event Word Template** provided  
- Your **Table Coach** will rotate to guide, challenge, and unblock you  

👉 **Hackathon Template**  
Download and use the official **MCT Hack Event Template (Word)** to capture:
- Team Name & Table Number  
- Problem or Opportunity Statement  
- Pain Points & Inefficiencies  
- Approach Options  
- Chosen AI/Microsoft Solution & Why  
- Short Summary & Key Insights  

✅ **The completed template is the final required submission for judging.**

---

## 🏆 Evaluation Criteria

Final submissions are required to qualify and will be assessed on:

- **Strategic Alignment** – Does this matter to MCTs?  
- **Quantifiable Value** – Time saved, effort reduced, quality improved  
- **Community Impact** – Applicability across the MCT community  
- **Sustainability & Scalability**  
- **Clarity of Outcome**  

*AI will score first; judges will review.*

---

## 📦 Final Output (Required)

By the end of the hack, your team will submit:

- ✅ A **clear MCT‑focused problem statement**  
- ✅ An **AI‑driven solution concept** (Copilot, Agent, Automation, or Workflow)  
- ✅ A **short summary with key insights** captured in the Word template
